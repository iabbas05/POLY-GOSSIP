# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Bahrain Polytechnic students. Visitors can browse public content lightly; verified students participate with profiles or through the separate Poly Gossip submission flow.

## Product Purpose

Gossip BH is a campus social community combining short public posts, campus conversation, moderated Poly Gossip submissions, student discounts, and useful university information.

## Positioning

One student-only campus space that combines X-style discussion with a separately moderated Poly Gossip identity and verified student offers.

## Operating Context

The primary use case is frequent mobile browsing between classes. The web app is installable to the home screen and is intended to precede native iOS and Android apps.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- English interface.
- Registration is limited to verified `@student.polytechnic.bh` email addresses.
- Public-profile posts can contain text, up to four images, or one video up to ten seconds.
- Poly Gossip submissions can contain text and images, are visible to admins, and become public under the Poly Gossip name only after an admin reply.
- Likes expose the count publicly, while the list of people who liked remains private to the post owner.
- No direct messages and no blocking.
- Super Admin and Normal Admin roles manage moderation and operations on the separate admin domain.

## Brand Commitments

Use the established blue Polytechnic-inspired Gossip identity, the half-white/half-blue G mark, and the word GOSSIP. Preserve the existing logo and install icons.

## Evidence on Hand

- Production application at `https://gossipbh.com`.
- Existing logo and PWA icon assets in the repository.
- User-provided mobile interface reference emphasizing an app-like header, quick tabs, compact composer, separate post cards, and fixed bottom navigation.

## Product Principles

- Feel native and immediately understandable on a phone.
- Keep campus conversation easy to scan and quick to join.
- Preserve the clear boundary between public profiles and moderated Poly Gossip identity.
- Make safety and moderation part of the product behavior, not visual clutter.
